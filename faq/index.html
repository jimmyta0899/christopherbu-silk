Sourcing silk from Yizhou, answered.

The questions our B2B buyers ask most, with direct answers. For anything else, contact our team.

Where is the fabric sourced?

All mulberry silk fabric is sourced from Yizhou, Guangxi, China — known as the world's raw silk capital, with 30+ years of sericulture and degumming expertise.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

There is no universal fixed MOQ — trial rolls, custom widths and samples may be available subject to material, mill and schedule review. The final MOQ and any organic status are confirmed in the written quotation.

What are the export terms?

Samples ship DHL worldwide or via designated courier. Bulk orders ship FOB Nansha / FOB Yantian / FOB Qinzhou / FCA Yizhou (preferred). Payment is T/T 30% deposit, 70% against Bill of Lading.

Are OEKO-TEX or GOTS certifications available?

Yes. GOTS, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II, China Organic and quality-system files are held by our local mill in Yizhou and available for qualified buyer review within each document's listed scope. Certificate numbers are provided with your quotation and shipping documents.

Do you supply GOTS-certified organic silk?

Yes. Our organic mulberry silk is certified 100% organic across the full process, covering organic raw silk, greige yarns and greige fabrics. The license number and validity are provided with your quotation.

What products do you supply?

Nine core silk categories: mulberry raw silk yarn (20/22D white, natural dupion, luxury and GOTS-certified organic silk), greige fabric, dupioni, satin, jacquard luo, georgette, habotai (pongee), silk stretch satin (silk-spandex) and blended silk (silk-cotton / silk-linen) — supplied greige or dyed to order.

Who do you supply?

We work with mills, brands and ateliers on bulk orders from 50m (samples are available for smaller needs).
