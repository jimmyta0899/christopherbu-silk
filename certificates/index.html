Certifications & compliance

Silk that meets the standards your buyers require — verified by certificates held by our local mill in Yizhou.

OEKO-TEX® STANDARD 100 (Class II)

Confirms the fabric has been tested for harmful substances and is safe for direct skin contact — relevant for apparel, bridal and couture applications.

Certificate number provided on quotation and shipping documents.

GOTS (Global Organic Textile Standard)

Covers the organic status and processing of our mulberry silk, from fibre to finished fabric — including 100% organic silk processed through boiling, pre-treatment, spinning and weaving, supplied as raw silk, greige yarns and greige fabrics.

Certificate number and license details provided on quotation.

China Organic & quality-system files

China Organic certification and quality-system files are also available for qualified buyer review, alongside GOTS and OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II. Each file is shared within its own listed document scope.

Available for qualified buyer review within each document's listed scope.

Destination-market compliance

California Proposition 65, EU textile-safety and other destination-market requirements are reviewed against product-specific test reports, and confirmed in writing before order approval — so the certification position for your market is clear before you commit.

Quality inspection

Every order is inspected for grade, denier/momme, width and hand before shipment. We confirm specification against current mill availability before quotation — so what you order is what ships.

Verify with the issuing authority

Certificate and licence numbers are provided on the written quotation and shipping documents. You can verify the standard body directly:

  • OEKO-TEX® label check — verify a certified company / label.
  • GOTS public licence database — look up a GOTS licence by company or licence number.
  • Raw silk grading follows GB/T 1797-2008 (Chinese national standard for raw silk).
