The plan tells the loom which warp threads to lift and which to leave down for each row of weft. The raised and lowered threads form the pattern as the cloth grows. Because the motif is in the weave, it survives washing and wear the way a print or a surface embroidery cannot. For example, a gold line woven into a green ground stays level through repeated wear, where a stitched line would lift and catch. For a silk jacquard, the base yarn is mulberry silk, and the motif is woven in the same silk or a contrasting yarn.
