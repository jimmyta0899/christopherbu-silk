Sourcing Silk for US Ateliers: Lead Times, MOQs and What Nobody Tells You

If you're a small atelier in Los Angeles or New York, ordering 200m of custom jacquard from China sounds risky. Here's exactly how the timeline works, what FOB Qinzhou actually means for your landed cost, and when a swatch book is enough to start.

Lead time reality: 25 days, not 7

Bulk silk orders ship in 20–25 days, not a week. Factor in weaving, inspection and ocean freight. Planning around the real timeline is the difference between a gown delivered on time and a missed deadline.

Understanding FOB Qinzhou vs. landed cost

FOB Qinzhou means the supplier covers cost and freight to the named port; you cover insurance and onward shipping. Your landed cost adds freight, duty and brokerage — budget for them, and the quote stops surprising you.

MOQ: small batch 50m, custom jacquard 200m

Plain weaves start at a 50m small batch. Custom jacquard requires 200m because of the setup involved in cutting a new card. Know your volume before you enquire — it determines what is actually possible.

The swatch book safety net

A $25 swatch book (credited on your first order) lets you verify hand, weight and color before committing to a bulk order. It is the cheapest insurance you can buy against a costly rework.
