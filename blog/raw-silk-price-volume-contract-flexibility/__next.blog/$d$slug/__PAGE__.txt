Does this term say what the goods are, or does it say when, how much and under what conditions the goods arrive? A term that defines the goods belongs on the written quotation and should be fixed. A term that describes the order belongs in the written confirmation before order approval and should stay flexible.\n\nA written quotation is the document that fixes the spec, the price and the acceptance evidence for one order. A conformance term is a contract term that says what the goods are, so it is fixed on the written quotation. An execution term is a contract term that says when, how much and under what conditions the goods arrive, so it is confirmed in writing before order approval. The two kinds of term sound similar on a contract, which is why buyers mix them up. They need different treatment.\n\nThe methodology is plain. Take each term in turn and ask two questions: which document would satisfy it, and when must that document exist. A term with a fixed answer and a document that exists at quotation is a conformance term, so lock it. A term whose value is set later, in the signed order documents, is an execution term, so leave it open and require it in writing before approval.
