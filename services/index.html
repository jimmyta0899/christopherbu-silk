Quality control

Four inspection stages keep every order within specification, from raw material to final shipment.

Stage 1

Raw material inspection

Screen silk against the confirmed material specification before production.

Stage 2

Semi-finished in-line inspection

Real-time check of weave integrity, texture and details during processing.

Stage 3

Finished goods inspection

Appearance, size, color and workmanship checks against the agreed acceptance criteria.

Stage 4

Pre-shipping final audit

Final recheck before delivery, with inspection records prepared for buyer review.
