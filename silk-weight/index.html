Five silk weights, and what each one actually decides

Last checked 2026-11-15

Quick answer

Momme is the weight of woven silk in g/m², and one momme is 4.340 g/m². These five pages cover the weights our range is actually specified at, each with the weaves that reach it and the weight of one running meter at 114 and 140 cm.

Last reviewed 2026-11-15

A weight on its own is not a specification. Two cloths can both be 19 momme and behave nothing alike, because momme measures mass per area and says nothing about construction. So each page below leads with the facts that belong to that weight only: which weaves are published at it, the weight of a running meter at each standard width, and the buying question the weight settles. If you want the unit itself explained, including why a greige figure and a finished figure are different numbers, start with the momme guide.

  • 6 momme · 26.04 g/m²

    6 Momme Silk Fabric

    6 momme silk weighs 26.04 g/m². In our range it is woven only as Habotai Silk, at 114 cm. One running meter weighs 0.0297 kg. It is the weight for lining, scarf and sheer-overlay work, where handling matters more than body.

  • 16 momme · 69.44 g/m²

    16 Momme Silk Fabric

    16 momme silk weighs 69.44 g/m². It is the top of the Habotai range and the entry weight for Dupioni Silk, Jacquard Luo Silk and Silk Stretch Satin. One meter at 140 cm weighs 0.0972 kg, at 114 cm 0.0792 kg. It is the usual floor for shirting and for a lining that has to hold a seam.

  • 19 momme · 82.46 g/m²

    19 Momme Silk Fabric

    19 momme silk weighs 82.46 g/m². It is the standard weight for Dupioni gowns and bridal, and the top of the 16 to 19 satin band used for sleepwear and shirts. One meter at 140 cm weighs 0.1154 kg, at 114 cm 0.0940 kg.

  • 22 momme · 95.48 g/m²

    22 Momme Silk Fabric

    22 momme silk weighs 95.48 g/m². It sits in the 22 to 25 satin band used for high-end bedding, and it is 15.8 percent heavier than 19 momme. One meter at 140 cm weighs 0.1337 kg, at 114 cm 0.1088 kg. Ask whether the figure is greige or finished before you compare.

  • 30 momme · 130.20 g/m²

    30 Momme Silk Fabric

    30 momme silk weighs 130.20 g/m². It is the top of the Satin Silk and Jacquard Luo Silk ranges, and past the 28 momme ceiling of Silk Stretch Satin. One meter at 140 cm weighs 0.1823 kg, at 114 cm 0.1484 kg. It is specified where the cloth has to hold a shape by itself.

Turning a weight into an order quantity

Fabric is quoted per meter or per yard, and only raw silk yarn is sold by weight. The moment a shipment crosses a border, though, the charge is calculated on kilograms, so the per-meter figures above are what a cost sheet needs. Two tools do the arithmetic:
