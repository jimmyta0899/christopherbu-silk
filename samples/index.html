Free sample questions

Are the samples really free?
The sample itself is free — we make no material charge for mini samples once we have confirmed availability of the quality you ask for. The buyer covers courier, customs and related international shipping, normally by DHL or a designated courier.
Do I have to pay for the fabric to see it first?
No. Silk is judged by hand, weight and luster, which no photograph reproduces. We send mini samples free of material charge so you can verify hand and colour before committing to bulk. Full-size sample yardage and finished apparel are chargeable.
How are samples shipped?
By DHL worldwide or a designated courier, dispatched once availability is confirmed. Shipping, customs and any related charges are borne by the buyer.
Can I request several qualities in one request?
Yes. Select every quality you are considering — raw silk yarn, greige fabric, dupioni, satin, stretch satin, jacquard luo, georgette, habotai or silk blends — and we will confirm availability across the set in one reply.
Can I get a sample in my own colour?
Standard shade card colours are the fastest route. Custom colours are dyed to order, so a dyed sample is quoted separately once we have your reference — Pantone code, lab dip target or a physical sample.
