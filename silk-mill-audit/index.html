Silk mill audit: the desk work before the visit

Quick answer

A mill audit inspects the floor. Certificate scope, GOTS license status, grading standard and destination-market compliance are verified on paper, then confirmed in writing before order approval. Close those rows before you travel.

Last reviewed 2026-11-28

A visit answers what can be seen: the winding area, the labels on the bales, the lot mark on the floor. It cannot answer whether a GOTS license is current or which products a certificate covers. Those are read from documents, so the two checks belong at different desks.

The sequence

Work down the list in order. Each step names the document that closes it.

  1. 1. Settle the entity in writing

    The written quotation fixes the legal entity, the authorized contact and the production origin. These belong to the entity and verification group of the five-group framework.

  2. 2. List the certification scopes offered for review

    GOTS, OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 Class II, China Organic and quality-system files, each within the scope printed on its own document, for qualified buyer review.

  3. 3. Verify the numbers at the desk

    Check the OEKO-TEX number through the public label check and the GOTS license through the public license database. Record the result before order approval.

  4. 4. Tie each grade claim to a lot

    Raw silk grading follows GB/T 1797-2008. Ask for the grading certificate per lot and tie each grade claim to a lot mark.

  5. 5. Review destination-market compliance

    California Prop 65 and EU textile safety are reviewed against product-specific test reports and confirmed in writing before order approval.

  6. 6. Close the rows, then visit

    A row closes only when the document named in it exists and the moment named in it has passed. Raise every open row with the seller in writing before the visit.

One rule for closing a row

A row closes only when the document named in it exists, and the moment named in it has passed. Ask two questions of each field: what paper satisfies this check, and when must that paper exist. A field with no document and no date stays open. Raise every open row with the seller in writing before the visit.

Common questions

Can a mill audit replace the certificate checks?
No. A site visit proves what is physically present. Certificate scope, license status and destination-market compliance are verified through public databases and product-specific test reports, then confirmed in writing before order approval.
When does a buyer get the certificate numbers?
With the written quotation and the shipping documents. Those are the documents checked against the OEKO-TEX label check and the GOTS public license database.
Which certification scopes can a buyer review?
GOTS, OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100 Class II, China Organic and quality-system files, each within that document's listed scope. Raw silk grading follows GB/T 1797-2008.
