Three primary silk products

Our core supply spans the three grades and forms buyers request most: raw silk for weaving, greige fabric for finishing, and douppioni for structured couture.

Mulberry Raw Silk (Filature Silk)

We supply 20/22 denier mulberry raw silk (filature silk) in 4A, 5A and 6A grades, sourced directly from Yizhou, China — the world's largest raw silk producing region.

Greige Silk Fabric

We supply undyed greige mulberry silk fabric from Yizhou partner mills, ideal for brands and mills that want full control over dyeing, printing and finishing.

Douppioni Silk

Douppioni silk is a textured mulberry silk with a distinctive slubbed surface, widely used for evening gowns, bridal wear and structured couture garments. Available in 19, 23, 30 and 40 momme.

Yizhou: the world's largest raw silk origin.

Yizhou, in Guangxi, China, has held the country's top ranking for mulberry field area, cocoon output and raw silk production for over two decades. Its raw silk regularly reaches 5A and 6A grades, the highest quality tiers, and supplies leading luxury brands worldwide.
