Three primary silk products

Our core supply spans the three grades and forms buyers request most: raw silk for weaving, greige fabric for finishing, and dupioni for structured couture.

All of it comes from Yizhou, in Guangxi, China — the world's largest raw silk origin, holding the country's top ranking for mulberry field area, cocoon output and raw silk production for over two decades. Its raw silk regularly reaches 5A and 6A grades, the highest quality tiers.

Sourcing silk from Yizhou, answered.

The questions our B2B buyers ask most — sourcing, MOQ, export terms and certification.

Where is the fabric sourced?

All mulberry silk fabric is sourced from Yizhou, Guangxi, China — known as the world's raw silk capital, with 30+ years of sericulture and degumming expertise.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

Swatches start at $25 (credited on your first order). Bulk MOQ ranges from 50m for plain weaves to 200m for custom jacquard.

What are the export terms?

Swatches ship FOB Shanghai (5–7 days). Bulk orders ship FOB Qinzhou or FCA Yizhou (20–25 days). Payment is T/T 30% deposit, 70% against Bill of Lading.

Are OEKO-TEX or GOTS certifications available?

Yes, held by our local mill in Yizhou. Certificate numbers are provided with your quotation and shipping documents.

What products do you supply?

Three core products: mulberry raw silk (filature silk) in 20/22 denier, greige silk fabric (undyed, ready for your own dyeing and finishing), and dupioni silk with its signature slub texture.

Who do you supply?

We work with mills, brands and ateliers on bulk orders from 50m (swatches are available for smaller needs).
