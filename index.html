Our Primary Silk Products

Our core supply spans the three forms buyers request most: raw silk for weaving, greige fabric for finishing, and dupioni for structured couture.

All of it comes from Yizhou, in Guangxi, China — the world's largest raw silk origin, holding the country's top ranking for mulberry field area, cocoon output and raw silk production for over two decades. Its raw silk is graded from 4A to 6A, with 5A and 6A the highest tiers.

Silk Fabrics

Greige Silk Fabric

Greige silk fabric is undyed, unfinished mulberry silk straight off the loom — a state, not a variety. Its momme follows the base weave you choose, so we quote it to your weave and specification.

Source →

Satin Silk Fabric

Satin silk is a lustrous, smooth-faced mulberry silk with a soft sheen and fluid drape, supplied greige or dyed to order from a standard shade card or your custom color.

Source →

Jacquard Luo Silk

Jacquard luo is a mulberry silk with motifs woven directly into the cloth on a jacquard loom, supplied greige or dyed to order, with custom pattern development for couture and occasionwear.

Source →

Georgette Silk

Georgette is a sheer, crinkled mulberry silk with a slight crepe texture and flowing drape, supplied greige or dyed to order for scarves, summer wear and airy garments.

Source →

Habotai Silk

Habotai (also known as pongee or China silk) is a plain-weave mulberry silk with a crisp, firm hand and a subtle texture, supplied greige or dyed to order for linings, scarves and light shirts.

Source →

Silk Stretch Satin

Silk stretch satin is a satin-weave blend of mulberry silk with spandex, keeping the luminous satin face while adding stretch and recovery, supplied greige or dyed to order from 14 to 28 momme.

Source →

Silk Blend (Silk-Cotton / Silk-Linen)

Blended silk combines mulberry silk with cotton or linen, pairing silk's sheen and softness with the comfort and breathability of natural fibers.

Source →

Dupioni Silk

Dupioni silk is a textured mulberry silk with a distinctive slubbed surface, widely used for evening gowns, bridal wear and structured couture garments. Available in 16 and 19 momme, up to 21–22 momme.

Sourcing silk from Yizhou, answered.

The questions our B2B buyers ask most — sourcing, MOQ, export terms and certification.

Where is the fabric sourced?

All mulberry silk fabric is sourced from Yizhou, Guangxi, China — known as the world's raw silk capital, with 30+ years of sericulture and degumming expertise.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

There is no universal fixed MOQ — trial rolls, custom widths and samples may be available subject to material, mill and schedule review. The final MOQ and any organic status are confirmed in the written quotation.

What are the export terms?

Samples ship DHL worldwide or via designated courier. Bulk orders ship FOB Nansha / FOB Yantian / FOB Qinzhou / FCA Yizhou (preferred). Payment is T/T 30% deposit, 70% against Bill of Lading.

Are OEKO-TEX or GOTS certifications available?

Yes. GOTS, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II, China Organic and quality-system files are held by our local mill in Yizhou and available for qualified buyer review within each document's listed scope. Certificate numbers are provided with your quotation and shipping documents.

Do you supply GOTS-certified organic silk?

Yes. Our organic mulberry silk is certified 100% organic across the full process, covering organic raw silk, greige yarns and greige fabrics. The license number and validity are provided with your quotation.

What products do you supply?

Nine core silk categories: mulberry raw silk yarn (20/22D white, natural dupion, luxury and GOTS-certified organic silk), greige fabric, dupioni, satin, jacquard luo, georgette, habotai (pongee), silk stretch satin (silk-spandex) and blended silk (silk-cotton / silk-linen) — supplied greige or dyed to order.

Who do you supply?

We work with mills, brands and ateliers on bulk orders from 50m (samples are available for smaller needs).
