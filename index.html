Raw Silk and Fabrics, sourced from the world's Silk Capital.

Christopher Bu is the couture house behind some of the world's most-watched red-carpet silk gowns. Now the brand brings that same eye for silk to its own line of Yizhou mulberry raw silk, greige fabric and dupioni — for textile mills, brands and designers worldwide.

Our Primary Silk Products

Our core supply spans the three forms buyers request most: raw silk for weaving, greige fabric for finishing, and dupioni for structured couture.

All of it comes from Yizhou, in Guangxi, China — the world's largest raw silk origin, holding the country's top ranking for mulberry field area, cocoon output and raw silk production for over two decades. Its raw silk regularly reaches 5A and 6A grades, the highest quality tiers.

“世界桑蚕看中国，中国桑蚕看宜州” — World sericulture looks to China; China's sericulture looks to Yizhou.

Sourcing silk from Yizhou, answered.

The questions our B2B buyers ask most — sourcing, MOQ, export terms and certification.

Where is the fabric sourced?

All mulberry silk fabric is sourced from Yizhou, Guangxi, China — known as the world's raw silk capital, with 30+ years of sericulture and degumming expertise.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

There is no universal fixed MOQ — trial rolls, custom widths and swatches may be available subject to material, mill and schedule review. The final MOQ and any organic status are confirmed in the written quotation.

What are the export terms?

Swatches ship DHL worldwide or via designated courier. Bulk orders ship FOB Nansha / FOB Yantian / FOB Qinzhou / FCA Yizhou (preferred). Payment is T/T 30% deposit, 70% against Bill of Lading.

Are OEKO-TEX or GOTS certifications available?

Yes. GOTS, OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Class II, China Organic and quality-system files are held by our local mill in Yizhou and available for qualified buyer review within each document's listed scope. Certificate numbers are provided with your quotation and shipping documents.

Do you supply GOTS-certified organic silk?

Yes. Our organic mulberry silk is certified 100% organic across the full process, covering organic raw silk, greige yarns and greige fabrics. The license number and validity are provided with your quotation.

What products do you supply?

Nine core silk categories: mulberry raw silk yarn (20/22D white, natural dupion, luxury and GOTS-certified organic silk), greige fabric, dupioni, satin, jacquard luo, georgette, pongee, silk stretch satin (silk-spandex) and blended silk (silk-cotton / silk-linen) — supplied greige or dyed to order.

Who do you supply?

We work with mills, brands and ateliers on bulk orders from 50m (swatches are available for smaller needs).
