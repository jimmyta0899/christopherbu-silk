Frequently asked questions

What is 20/22D raw silk yarn?

20/22D is filature silk measured in the 20–22 denier range, the standard starting point for silk crepes, satins and fine weaving. Denier measures yarn weight; momme measures the weight of woven cloth. Buyers use denier when buying yarn and momme when buying fabric.

Do you supply deniers other than 20/22D?

20/22D is the standard and 13/19D are also held in regular supply. Other specifications, including finer and coarser deniers, can be offered on request — state the denier and grade you need and we confirm availability, packing and price in the written quotation.

Which HS code applies to raw silk yarn?

Raw silk (not thrown) is classified under HS heading 5002 in the Harmonized System. The exact subheading that applies to a shipment depends on the goods and destination, so we confirm it in the written quotation and shipping documents rather than stating a fixed code for every order.

What do the 4A, 5A and 6A grades mean for raw silk from Yizhou?

Chinese raw silk is graded from 4A to 6A. 4A is the standard commercial grade used for regular weaving, while 5A and 6A are the highest quality tiers. Yizhou, in Guangxi, has held China's top ranking for mulberry field area, cocoon output and raw silk production for over two decades, and its raw silk regularly reaches the 5A and 6A tiers. Grade availability for a given variety is confirmed in the written quotation.

How is raw silk yarn packed for export?

Raw silk yarn is packed in export cartons or bales, with 60 kg per bale as the typical reference and 5 kg bundles for luxury silk yarn. Final packing is confirmed against the order and destination before shipment.

What is the minimum order quantity for raw silk yarn?

Raw silk is sold by weight, not by the metre, and there is no universal fixed MOQ. Reference minimums vary by variety — for example, natural dupion silk yarn is typically quoted around 10 kg and luxury silk yarn around 5 kg — and the final MOQ is confirmed in the written quotation.

Can raw silk yarn be supplied dyed, or only greige?

Both. Yarn and fabric can be supplied greige (undyed) or dyed to order — we offer a standard shade card and custom color matching. Printed options are not listed in this specification.

What are the payment and shipping terms for bulk raw silk?

Bulk orders ship FOB Nansha, FOB Yantian, FOB Qinzhou or FCA Yizhou (preferred), with samples sent by DHL worldwide or a designated courier. Payment is T/T — 30% deposit and 70% against Bill of Lading. Full terms are on our export terms page.

Can I get samples before placing a bulk order?

Yes. Free mini samples may be supplied after availability is confirmed, and full-size samples are chargeable. See our free sample service and request a quotation with your denier, grade and intended use.

What does your written quotation include?

Every written quotation and order confirmation covers the items in our acceptance framework: legal entity and business registration, grade and quality tier, evenness, cleanness and neatness, lot mark and traceability, grading certificate, sample and test references, price and payment terms, incoterms, lead time, packing, HS classification, certification, certificate number and destination-market compliance. Each item is either stated in the written quotation or confirmed in writing before order approval.

How can I verify the grade or certificate of a silk shipment?

The grading certificate and certificate number are provided with the written quotation and shipping documents, and certification is confirmed in writing before order approval. The issuing authority can be used to check a licence or label, and we can arrange buyer verification when source documents are shared.

When are the final specification details confirmed?

Specification values are set per order. They are stated in the written quotation and any remaining details are confirmed in writing before order approval, so both parties work from the same document before production begins.
