Wholesale silk fabric from China: how a bulk order actually runs

Nine standing constructions from local mills in Yizhou, dyed to order on either a standard shade card or a custom match, shipped FOB or FCA with inspection records. This page sets out the seven steps from enquiry to container, the terms we quote, and the questions that decide whether two wholesale quotations are actually comparable.

Nine constructions

Raw silk yarn, greige, dupioni, satin, jacquard, georgette, habotai, stretch satin and silk blends — each with its own specification page.

Two colour routes

A standard shade card, or a custom match to your sample or reference. Greige is also supplied undyed for buyers with their own dyehouse.

FOB or FCA

Nansha, Yantian and Qinzhou for FOB; FCA Yizhou preferred, so the inland leg stays visible in your landed cost.

Seven steps from enquiry to container

01

Enquiry and specification

Send the garment or end use, the construction you have in mind, quantity, target market and any test requirements. If you only know the garment, say so — matching a construction to a silhouette is the part we are better at than a price list is.

02

Construction matching

We come back with the weave, the momme band measured on finished cloth, the width, and an honest note on what will not work at your quantity. This is where most orders are actually saved — a construction that cannot hold your seam is cheaper to reject now than after bulk.

03

Sampling

Mini fabric samples are supplied without a material charge once availability is confirmed; the buyer bears courier, customs and related international shipping costs. Full-size fabric and finished garments are chargeable, and any sample-fee credit against a later bulk order applies only when stated in the written quotation.

04

Quotation

The quotation states grade, momme measured on the finished side, width, colour route, quantity, unit price, lead-time estimate and the trade term. If any of those is missing, ask — a quotation without a width or a momme basis is not comparable to another supplier's.

05

Colour approval

Two routes: a standard shade from the colour card, or a custom match to a submitted sample or reference. Custom matching needs an approved lab dip before bulk, and the approved dip is what QC checks against — not the original reference.

06

Bulk production and QC

15–25 working days is a planning estimate for eligible regular bulk orders, counted after sample, specification, deposit and material availability are confirmed. Inspection runs in four stages from raw material to pre-shipping final audit, with records prepared for buyer review.

07

Shipping and documents

FOB Nansha, Yantian or Qinzhou, or FCA Yizhou, which is our preferred term because it keeps the inland leg visible. Export documentation, packing list and inspection records travel with the shipment.

Why a couture house quotes your fabric

Most wholesale silk is quoted by a trading desk that never cuts the cloth. We quote fabric we also cut in the atelier, which changes what gets flagged. A cloth that passes a test report can still photograph grey under flash, crease at the hip after twenty minutes of sitting, or lose its edge on a bias seam — and those are the failures that cost a production run, not the ones on the inspection sheet.

It also changes what we refuse. We will tell you when a construction your brief calls for cannot hold at your quantity, rather than quoting it and letting the sample stage discover the problem. That conversation is cheaper for both of us.

Wholesale questions

What is the minimum order for wholesale silk fabric?

There is no universal fixed minimum. Small trial rolls, custom widths and samples may be available subject to material, mill and schedule review. The final minimum depends on the construction, the width and whether the colour is a standard shade or a custom match, and it is confirmed in the written quotation rather than published as a single number.

Do you supply free samples?

Mini fabric samples can be supplied without a material charge once availability is confirmed. The buyer bears all courier, customs and related international shipping costs, usually by DHL or a designated courier. Full-size silk fabrics and finished apparel are chargeable, and any sample fee credit against a later bulk order applies only when it is stated in the quotation or pro forma invoice.

Can you dye silk fabric to a custom colour?

Yes, on both tracks — standard shade card colours and custom matching to a submitted sample or specified reference. Custom work requires an approved lab dip before bulk, and the approved dip becomes the reference for inspection. Greige fabric is also supplied undyed for buyers running their own dyehouse. Colour is what we quote for; surface effects beyond dyeing are a separate conversation and we will say plainly whether they are available.

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

We work with local mills in Yizhou, and we are a couture house with its own design and production standard. In practice that means the cloth we quote is the cloth we cut — the specification is written by people who see it on a fitting model, not only on a test report. Our export and sourcing operations run through Beijing Qingshanyimu Trading Co., Ltd.

How long does bulk production take?

15–25 working days is a planning estimate for eligible regular bulk orders. The clock starts once the sample, specification, deposit and material availability are all confirmed — not at the date of enquiry. Custom weaving, custom colour matching and jacquard pattern development each add their own lead time, and the binding schedule is the one in the signed order documents.

Do you supply OEKO-TEX or GOTS certified silk?

Yes, and the certificates are held by the local mill rather than issued to us. We provide the certificate number and validity on quotation so you can verify them with the issuing body. We do not display certificate numbers on this website, because a badge you cannot check is worse than no badge.

Can you develop a custom weave, width or pattern?

Custom width and custom pattern development are available where the weave allows, and jacquard pattern development is part of the jacquard programme. What we will not do is accept a specification the mill cannot hold at your quantity — better to say so at the sampling stage than to ship a roll that fails your cutting room.

What are your shipping terms?

Bulk orders ship FOB Nansha, Yantian or Qinzhou, or FCA Yizhou, which is our preferred term. Payment is T/T 30% deposit with 70% against the bill of lading. Samples usually move by DHL worldwide or a designated courier.
